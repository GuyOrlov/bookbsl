Quick answers

BSL booking FAQs.

How far ahead should I book a BSL interpreter?

As early as possible. Availability can be limited for evenings, weekends, specialist work and large events. If your date is close, still ask — as we can still check current availability.

Do I always need two BSL interpreters?

No. It all depends on duration, intensity, subject, format, breaks, stage work and whether the session is recorded or livestreamed. Give full details so the agency can make the best recommendation.

Is a Communication Support Worker the same as a BSL interpreter?

No. They are different roles. Book the professional that matches the Deaf person's needs and the setting.

Does BookBSL make the booking automatically?

Not yet. This version checks your information and prepares a clear enquiry. You can then send it to cSeeker for availability, advice and a quote.

Does BookBSL store my answers?

No server storage is used by this checklist. Your answers stay on the page while you use it. They are only shared if you choose to copy them or open your email app to send the enquiry.

BSL interpreter booking FAQ

How to book a BSL interpreter in the UK.

Simple answers for organisations booking British Sign Language and Deaf communication support.

How do I book a BSL interpreter?

Start by confirming the Deaf person’s communication preference. Then collect the date, start and finish times, venue or online platform, number of people needing support, recording plans and any preparation material. BookBSL turns those details into a clear enquiry that you can send to cSeeker for availability and a quote.

How much does a BSL interpreter cost?

The price can depend on the booking length, location, travel, preparation, time of day, number of interpreters or communication professionals required, and whether the work is recorded or livestreamed. BookBSL does not invent a fixed price; use the checklist and request a quote for the actual booking.

Can I book an online or remote BSL interpreter?

Yes. BookBSL supports online, in-person and hybrid bookings. For a remote booking, add the platform such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams. The joining link can be supplied later if it is not ready yet.

Do events and conferences need two BSL interpreters?

Sometimes. Duration, complexity, breaks, stage work, recording and livestreaming can affect whether one interpreter is suitable or a team is recommended. BookBSL flags longer or recorded bookings so cSeeker can confirm the appropriate team.

Can BookBSL help with workplace and Access to Work interpreter bookings?

Yes. The checklist can organise the practical information for workplace meetings, training, interviews and other work-related BSL interpreter requests. Access to Work funding approval, budgets and reimbursement remain separate from BookBSL and should be checked with the relevant parties.

Can I request an NRCPD-registered BSL interpreter?

If registration or a particular professional qualification is required for your booking, include that requirement in the enquiry. cSeeker can then check the booking needs and availability of an appropriate communication professional.

What if the Deaf person does not use BSL?

Do not assume that every Deaf person wants a BSL interpreter. They may prefer speech-to-text, a lipspeaker, Deaf Relay or another form of communication support. BookBSL asks you to confirm the person’s preference before the enquiry is sent.

What information should I send before the booking?

Useful preparation can include an agenda, slides, scripts, speaker names, specialist vocabulary, venue access information and recording or livestream details. If these are not ready, you can still begin the enquiry and send the remaining information later.
